Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

CPE Radar (“we,” “us”) runs cperadar.com and the “Free CPEs This Week” newsletter. CPE Radar is the operator, based in California, USA. Questions: hello@cperadar.com.

We don’t have user accounts, we don’t sell your information, and we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we collect

Newsletter. When you subscribe, our newsletter provider, beehiiv, collects your email address and standard email data, like whether an email was delivered, opened, or clicked. beehiiv’s privacy policy: beehiiv.com/privacy

Submit an Event form. We collect the event details you enter: title, host, date and time, format, CPE credits, and registration link. We also collect your email address if you give it.

Sponsor form. We collect your company name, work email, the sponsorship you’re interested in, and your message.

Emails to us. If you write to hello@cperadar.com, we get your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Technical data. Our host, Cloudflare, processes the standard information that comes with every web request: your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the time of the request. Cloudflare uses it to deliver the site, keep it secure, and prevent abuse. For example, we limit how many forms one network can submit, using a scrambled (hashed) version of the IP address that is kept for about an hour. Cloudflare’s privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

How we use it

  • To send you the newsletter you asked for.
  • To review and list events you submit, and to contact you about your submission.
  • To reply to sponsorship inquiries.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the service providers that run CPE Radar for us:

  • beehiiv sends the newsletter.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and forwards our email.
  • GitHub stores form submissions in a private repository.
  • Google (Gmail) receives email sent to hello@cperadar.com.

We don’t sell or rent personal information. We don’t “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising either. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Event details you submit, other than your email address, may be published on the calendar and in the newsletter. Publishing them is the purpose of the form.

Cookies and tracking

cperadar.com doesn’t set advertising or analytics cookies and doesn’t load third-party tracking scripts. Our newsletter emails may use beehiiv’s standard open and click tracking. The beehiiv subscribe page is run by beehiiv and covered by beehiiv’s policies.

Do Not Track. We don’t track you across other websites, so we treat every visitor the same whether or not their browser sends a Do Not Track signal. We don’t allow third parties to collect personal information about your online activity over time and across websites through our site.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every newsletter email.
  • To access, correct, or delete information you’ve sent us, email hello@cperadar.com and we’ll handle it within 30 days. Residents of California, the EU/UK, and other places with privacy laws can use this address to exercise any rights those laws give them.

How long we keep it

We keep subscriber data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep form submissions and emails as long as we need them to handle your request and keep basic business records, and we delete them sooner if you ask.

Children

CPE Radar is meant for working professionals. It isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

International visitors

CPE Radar is based in the United States, and our service providers may process data in the U.S. and other countries. We rely on your consent when you subscribe, and on our legitimate interest in answering your submissions and running the site.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above. If the change is significant, we’ll also mention it in the newsletter.
